How the research is evaluated

Evaluation boundaries matter more than polished charts.

Standardization is fitted only on training cohorts. Later entrants and incomplete outcome windows are excluded to reduce label censoring.

Why a high AUC isn't the whole answer

MODEL —
BASELINE —

We report ROC-AUC, Brier score and log loss. The baseline estimates every player's success rate using only historical cohort prevalence.

What's measured in Moneyball analysis?

Team NBA Four Factors, assists, steals, blocks, rebounds and three-point shooting come from observed season totals. League-relative comparisons are shown only if enough current teams were fetched. Per-player development probabilities are uncalibrated explorations, not demonstrated causal improvement.

What isn't ready?

A true NCAA-to-NBA selection model, on/off possession data, lineup fit, historical draft-eligible undrafted cohorts, injury forecasts, salary-adjusted trade value and validated real-time win probabilities all need further licensed data and chronological backtesting.
