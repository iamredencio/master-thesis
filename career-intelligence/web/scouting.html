Prediction vs explanation

01 / OBSERVED

Score, quarter, game clock and team IDs come from ESPN or NBA liveData. The evidence gate rejects missing timestamps or contradictory statuses.

02 / ESTIMATED

Only active, timed games get a provisional win-probability estimate using current score margin, remaining minutes and an explicit fixed variance. There is no calibrated pre-game rating yet.

03 / VERIFIED

Explanations are rendered from checked scoreboard fields—not generated free text. Injury and minutes forecasts are withheld without a verified provider.

Experimental in-game probabilities are research previews, not validated outcomes or betting advice. We do not issue pregame probabilities or future final scores without a calibrated dataset and evaluation. Read the model status ↗
